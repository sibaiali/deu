# 🇩🇪 German BFD + Psychiatry + B2/C1 Learning Platform

> **Live Website:** [https://sibaiali.github.io/deu/](https://sibaiali.github.io/deu/)

A complete, self-contained personal German learning and job-preparation platform for the Federal Volunteer Service (BFD) in acute psychiatry (UKGM Marburg) and the path to C1/C2 fluency.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

- **🏠 Heute & Tagesplan**: Daily streak, daily study goals, due SRS flashcard overview.
- **✨ Magischer Satz-Korrektor**: Grammar diagnosis & word-upgrade optimizer with accuracy ratings (100% celebration + XP) and 3 stylistic registers (Casual, Professional, C1/C2).
- **🧠 Anti-Übersetzungs-Trainer**: Monolingual German concept mapping, rapid real-life situational reactions, and false-friends buster (*no English intermediary steps!*).
- **🏢 Mein BFD (UKGM)**: Contract breakdown (420 € cash allowance + housing/food benefits), ward rules, team roles (*what you can do vs. what you cannot do*), and First-Day Survival Mode.
- **🃏 Vokabeln & SRS (220+ Cards)**: Multi-dimensional dictionary cards across 29 themes (Family, House, Going Out, Psychiatry, Hospital, Top 100 German Verbs) with SM-2 spaced repetition scheduling.
- **🎭 BFD-Simulationen**: Multi-turn clinical roleplays with instant 4-tier evaluation (*Basic B1*, *Natürlich*, *Professionell B2*, *C1-Register*).
- **🎙️ Sprechtrainer**: Timed spontaneous speaking challenges and native German Text-to-Speech (TTS).
- **💬 Was sage ich?**: Practical phrase transformer and *"Ich verstehe nicht!"* clinic emergency trainer.
- **📖 Grammatik (25 Lektionen)**: Comprehensive grammatical progression + *Der, Die, Das* master gender rules.
- **🧠 Psychologie verstehen**: Clinical psychiatry guide, trauma criteria, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, and verbal de-escalation models.
- **📑 Leseverstehen**: Real-world hospital notices, handover protocols, and case studies with comprehension drills.
- **🗺️ Praxis-Roadmaps**: Step-by-step guidance for Day 1–7, Month 1, and the 26 mandatory seminar days.
- **⚙️ Engineering Deutsch**: B2/C1 technical specifications, error analysis, and metrology.
- **💾 Offline-First & PWA**: Runs 100% offline with zero server dependencies via IndexedDB & LocalStorage.

## 🚀 Local Quick Start

Run on Windows:
```cmd
start_platform.bat
```
Or with Python:
```bash
python server.py
```
Open in browser: `http://localhost:3000`
